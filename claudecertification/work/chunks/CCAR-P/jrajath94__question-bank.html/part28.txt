<h3 id="bank-mocks-h50">M1-Q46 (V2-D5.3)</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p227" data-kind="prose"><strong>Scenario.</strong> A contract-signing agent prepares signature packets. A
wrong signature binds the company to bad terms.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p228" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> Which review shape fits?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p229" data-kind="prose">A) Do a post-action sampled review.
B) Do a pre-action approval. A qualified signer reviews the exact packet and terms. This happens before any signing.
C) Do not do a review. The agent is accurate.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p230" data-kind="prose">D) Do an annual review.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p231" data-kind="prose"><strong>Key.</strong> B. Decisive phrase: "A wrong signature binds the company to
bad terms." Why: signing is irreversible and high-consequence. Only
pre-action approval with full context can stop the harm. A reviews
after binding. Counterfactual: A wins for reversible low-stakes
packets.</p>


<h3 id="bank-mocks-h51">M1-Q47 (V2-D5.3)</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p234" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>

B) Sampled human review plus audit logging.

D) CEO approval per draft.</p>

real detection cheaply. Counterfactual: A wins at 20 drafts per day
with high stakes.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p238" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>

B) A review cadence: re-check the controls on a schedule.
C) A nicer chatbot avatar.

E) A bigger model.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p239" data-kind="prose"><strong>Answer.</strong> A, B</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p240" data-kind="prose"><strong>Key.</strong> A, B. Decisive phrase: "no named owner and no review

evidence to cadence. Name the missing links explicitly. C, D, and
E are not chain links. Counterfactual: none. Cosmetics never complete
compliance.</p>


<h3 id="bank-mocks-h53">M1-Q49 (V2-D5.4)</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p241" data-kind="prose"><strong>Scenario.</strong> A support bot stores EU customer tickets. The team sets
retention: tickets auto-delete after 3 years, sooner on customer
request.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p242" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> What makes this retention design sound?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p243" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) The 3-year number alone.
B) The retention rule tied to the requirement, with an owner, evidence
of enforcement, and a review cadence. Plus the on-request deletion
path.
C) Deleting everything after one day.
D) Keeping everything forever.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p244" data-kind="prose"><strong>Key.</strong> B. Decisive phrase: &quot;auto-delete after 3 years, sooner on
customer request.&quot; Why: retention is a link in the compliance chain:
requirement, control, owner, evidence, cadence. The deletion path
honors the request right. C destroys business evidence. D ignores the
requirement.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p245" data-kind="prose">Counterfactual: C wins only if a rule truly demands
one-day deletion.</p>


<h3 id="bank-mocks-h54">M1-Q50 (V2-D5.5)</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p247" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> What does responsible AI require?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p248" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) &quot;The model made the decision.&quot;
B) Traceability: Link the decision to the factors and data. Explain the decision to the applicant. Measure per-group fairness.
C) A higher confidence score.
D) Silence.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p249" data-kind="prose">The key is B. The decisive phrase is &quot;Applicants ask why.&quot; V2-D5.5 requires transparency. It requires explainability. It requires accountability. It requires traceability. The decision must link to its factors.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p250" data-kind="prose">Measure fairness per group. A and D give up responsibility. Opacity is never the answer.</p>

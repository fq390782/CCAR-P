<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p1131" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>

B) Residency violation: EU data must stay in the EU per the

not a control. The fix is regional placement plus the chain.
Counterfactual: A wins when no residency rule applies.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p223" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> What is the best action?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p224" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) Block the hotfix until the design review and ADR are done.
B) Ship the hotfix now, then write the ADR and hold the review in the post-incident window.
C) Run full discovery before the fix.
D) Wait for the architecture board's next meeting.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p225" data-kind="prose"><strong>Answer.</strong> B</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p227" data-kind="prose">The objective is to restore service fast. Do not lose the record.
4. Hard constraints: customers are down. The fix is ready. You must write the record.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p228" data-kind="prose">5. The system layer is lifecycle pragmatics.
6. Eliminate infeasible options: all four are executable.
7. Eliminate constraint-violating options: A lets downtime grow while the process runs.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p229" data-kind="prose">C runs discovery during an outage. D schedules the fix for later.
8. Compare on objective: B restores service now. B catches the record up after.
9.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p230" data-kind="prose">Hidden dependencies: the post-incident window must be real and scheduled. If not, you will never write the record.
10. Verify: B is the only answer. This is a single select question.</p>

<h3 id="bank-d6-h26">Q-D6-25 (V2-D6.5)</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p254" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> What is the best action?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p255" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) Keep all four reviews. Process is process.
B) Right-size the gates to the team: light checks that fit two people, with the full gates reserved for production or handoff changes.
C) Drop all gates permanently.
D) Add a fifth review for safety.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p256" data-kind="prose"><strong>Answer.</strong> B</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p257" data-kind="prose"><strong>Method walk (Steps 1-10).</strong>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p259" data-kind="prose">Eliminate infeasible: all four are executable.

8.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p260" data-kind="prose">Compare on objective: B scales the gates to the stakes and keeps the full gates where they matter.
9. Hidden dependencies: the team must define which changes count as production or handoff, or the reservation is vague.
10. Verify: B alone. Single select.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-d6-p261" data-kind="prose"><strong>Explanation.</strong>

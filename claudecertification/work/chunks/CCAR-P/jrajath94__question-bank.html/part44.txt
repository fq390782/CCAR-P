<h3 id="bank-mocks-h222">M4-Q17 (V2-D2.4)</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p984" data-kind="prose"><strong>Scenario.</strong> A customer support bot loads the full 100,000-token
product manual on every call. The bill is 8x the budget. Questions need
one chapter.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p985" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> What is the first fix?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p986" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) A larger window.
B) Retrieve the relevant chapter just in time instead of the full
manual.
C) A stronger tier.
D) Shorter manuals.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p987" data-kind="prose"><strong>Key.</strong> B. Decisive phrase: "Questions need one chapter... full
100,000-token manual." Why: growth is the fault. Retrieval is the fix.
Counterfactual: A wins when every question needs the whole manual and
the budget allows.</p>


<h3 id="bank-mocks-h223">M4-Q18 (V2-D2.4), Select TWO</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p988" data-kind="prose"><strong>Scenario.</strong> A news bot's context holds: all of today's articles, the
same correction notice pasted 5 times, and yesterday's articles too.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p989" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> Which TWO faults are present? Select TWO.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p990" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) Growth: far more than needed loads every call.
B) Duplication: the correction notice appears 5 times.
C) Loss: old turns vanish.
D) Exhaustion: the window errors.
E) Perfect context.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p991" data-kind="prose"><strong>Answer.</strong> A, B</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p992" data-kind="prose"><strong>Key.</strong> A, B. Decisive phrase: "all of today's articles... pasted 5
times... yesterday's articles too." Why: the load is growth. The 5x
notice is duplication. C and D have no evidence. Counterfactual: D
wins when the call errors on window size.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p994" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> Should the team keep prompt caching?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p995" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) No. 3,000 tokens is small.
B) Yes: the prefix is stable and the measured 92% hit rate clears the
break-even point by a wide margin.

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p996" data-kind="prose">D) Switch to response caching instead.</p>

identically.</p>

<h3 id="bank-mocks-h227">M4-Q22 (V2-D3.2)</h3>

<code>company</code> field in the user's profile JSON, which the user can edit.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p1011" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> What is wrong?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p1012" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) Nothing. The profile filters.
B) The profile field is user-editable, so it is a claim. The filter
must use the authenticated user&#x27;s company in code.
C) The bot needs a bigger model.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p1013" data-kind="prose">D) JSON is too slow.</p>

the field is server-set and uneditable.</p>

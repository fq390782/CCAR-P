<h3 id="bank-mocks-h178">M3-Q40 (V2-D4.5)</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p798" data-kind="prose"><strong>Scenario.</strong> A chat service's p99 latency is 14 s. The team optimizes

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p799" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> What is wrong?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p800" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) Nothing. The median improved.
B) The tail is the problem: p99 at 14 s means 1% of users wait
terribly. Optimize the tail (timeouts, retries, slow stages), not the
median.
C) The model is too small.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p801" data-kind="prose">D) Users complain too much.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p802" data-kind="prose"><strong>Key.</strong> B. The decisive phrase is &quot;p99 latency is 14 s.&quot; V2-D4.5 targets p95/p99 for interactive systems. Median work ignores the suffering tail. A wins when the SLA is on the median. A wins when users are happy.</p>

B) A measured threshold. For example, human-rated adequacy is 4/5 or more.

threshold.</p>


<h3 id="bank-mocks-h105">M2-Q34 (V2-D4.2)</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p472" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> What is necessary?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p473" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) Trust the judge.
B) Calibrate the judge against human labels on a sampled set before
trusting its grades.
C) A bigger judge.
D) More answers.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p474" data-kind="prose"><strong>Key.</strong> B.
The decisive phrase is &quot;never checked the judge against human grades.&quot;
The grading ladder puts code first.
Then the grading ladder puts the judge.
Then the grading ladder puts the human.
You calibrate judges against human labels.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p475" data-kind="prose">An uncalibrated judge is an unverified grader.
A wins never for grading.</p>

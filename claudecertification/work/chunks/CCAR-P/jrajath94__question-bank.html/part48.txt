<h3 id="bank-mocks-h262">M4-Q57 (V2-D6.4)</h3>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p1169" data-kind="prose"><strong>Scenario.</strong> A team chose prompt caching and recorded an ADR. A year
later the provider changes cache pricing.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p1170" data-kind="prose"><strong>Question.</strong> What happens next?</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p1171" data-kind="prose"><strong>Options.</strong>
A) Nothing.
B) The ADR's review criteria trigger re-evaluation: does caching still
pay at the new prices.
C) Delete the ADR.
D) Panic.</p>

<p id="sec-bank-mocks-p1172" data-kind="prose"><strong>Key.</strong> B. Decisive phrase: "the provider changes cache pricing."
Why: pricing was an ADR assumption. Changed assumptions reopen the
decision. Counterfactual: A wins never when assumptions break.</p>
